Taim turns passing thoughts into useful threads, reminders, and next steps without asking you to stop and organize your life.

01 : Around the house

Capture the detail before it disappears

Say “dishwasher tablets” while you are making coffee, or “call the plumber next Tuesday” as you walk past the leak. Keep the thought moving without opening another app.

Before : A small task lives in your head until it becomes a bigger one.

With Taim : The detail is captured and ready when you need it.

02 : Plans and appointments

Give the next step a place to land

A dentist appointment, a school form, or a dinner you promised to arrange. Speak the whole thought naturally and keep the date, people, and context together.

Before : Dates, messages, and reminders are scattered across the day.

With Taim : One clear thread shows what needs to happen next.

03 : The passing idea

Keep life open to better thoughts

A recipe to try, a place to visit, or a way to make the evening easier. Capture the idea in the moment and return to it when the moment is right.

Before : Good ideas arrive at the wrong time and vanish before you can use them.

With Taim : The idea stays close without demanding attention.
