01 : Midnight idea

The 2am insight doesn't disappear

That perfect colour direction hits in the dark. A texture combination appears mid-dream. Type it into Taim in five words. Wake up to a sorted, searchable note with all the context still attached.

Before : Write on your hand. Lose it by morning. Feel the sting.

With Taim : Idea stored, filed, and ready when you open your laptop.

02 : Spontaneous direction

The sidewalk colour you only see once

A perfect terracotta on the way to the coffee shop, a spatial layout seen in a gallery. You are not stopping to open a notes app. Say it into Taim. It lands exactly where it belongs.

Before : Screenshot graveyard. Photo roll with no context. Gone in the scroll.

With Taim : Voice note, location, and mood - one thread. Searchable forever.

03 : Creative decisions

The why stays with the what

"Changed the weight because the rhythm felt off" matters six months later when you're revisiting the piece. Taim keeps the reasoning, not just the result.

Before : Buried in a chat thread. Lost in a Notion page nobody revisits.

With Taim : Decision thread alive. Searchable the moment it matters again.
