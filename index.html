How it works

One note, then the thread keeps going.

Taim does not expect you to be more organized. It catches the way life already arrives and keeps the important details connected.

01

Capture the real thought

Speak or type like a person: fragmented, emotional, unfinished. Taim keeps the important pieces without forcing a form.

02

Sort it into life topics

Trips, friends, work, health, family, ideas. A thread becomes the living place where notes, tasks, and context stay together.

03

Turn it into motion

A reminder, a calendar hold, a message draft, a task, a check-in. The next useful action appears where it belongs.

04

Notice what repeats

When the same signal keeps returning, Taim can nudge you gently before the pattern becomes another thing you carry alone.

Privacy

Built to respect the parts of life that are personal.

Taim keeps context usable without turning your private life into product data. The goal is simple trust.

Audio is never stored

Voice is transcribed, then deleted. The recording is not kept around.

Sensitive data is redacted

Card numbers, IDs, and credentials are stripped before model processing.

No training on your life

Your thoughts, threads, and memories are not sold or used to train models.
