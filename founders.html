01 : After the call

Keep the promise beside the context

A partner call ends with three useful details and one thing you said you would send. Put them down once, while the conversation is still fresh.

Before : A message, a half-written note, and a promise you remember too late.

With Taim : The decision and the follow-up stay together.

02 : Product judgment

Remember why the choice was made

Capture the small observation that changes the direction of a feature. Months later, the reasoning is still there with the decision.

Before : The final answer survives. The useful argument behind it fades.

With Taim : The path stays understandable to the team.

03 : A day in motion

Leave the desk without losing the day

Ideas do not wait for a clean calendar. Speak one thought between rooms, trains, and calls, then return to it when there is room to think.

Before : The thought competes with everything else in your head.

With Taim : It is held safely until you can give it proper attention.
