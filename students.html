01 : Lecture to thread

Capture the insight, not just the slide

Your professor makes an off-slide point that will be on the exam. Whisper it to Taim. It becomes a searchable note attached to that week's lecture thread.

Before : Frantic typing, miss the next thing, have a half-baked note that makes no sense later.

02 : Deadline management

No more "I forgot when that was due"

Say "Essay due Friday in three weeks" and Taim creates the reminder, keeps the context, and surfaces the nudge at the right moment - not a panic-inducing red badge.

Before : Five apps, three calendars, one all-nighter, regret.

With Taim : One thread. One nudge. No panic.

03 : Study group coordination

Who, when, where - all in one thread

"Study group with Amir and Priya, Wednesday at 5, Library room B3, bring the case study." Taim holds all of it. One search brings it back when you need it.

Before : Scattered across Whatsapp, email, your head, and your friend's memory.

With Taim : Organized thread. Reminder set. Zero missed messages.
